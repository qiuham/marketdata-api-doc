# Execution Report

> Order acknowledgements, fills, amends, cancels and status answers

FIX
session: trading
35=8

Reports every state change on an order once the engine has accepted it. ExecType says what happened — new, replaced, cancelled, expired, traded, restated, or an answer to a status request — and OrdStatus says what state the order is now in. Fills carry LastPx, LastQty, the running CumQty and AvgPx, the trade identifier, and the maker/taker flag in LastLiquidityInd.

**Direction:** Kraken → client

***

**header** *required*

35=`8`

**37 - OrderID** `string`

Exchange-assigned order identifier generated by Kraken; the value to quote back on cancels, amends and status requests.

**Format:** Assigned by Kraken; quote it back on cancels, amends and status requests.

**11 - ClOrdID** `string` *required*

Client-assigned order identifier, as sent by the client. On US Perps it is a positive integer of at most 19 digits.

**Format:** Positive integer, up to 19 digits, e.g. a microsecond timestamp.

**41 - OrigClOrdID** `string`

ClOrdID of the previous action on the order. Sent on cancel and amend reports.

Required: Conditional

**17 - ExecID** `string` *required*

Unique identifier of this execution report. Treat it as opaque: do not parse it or derive it from ClOrdID. On US Perps the New (`150=0`) acknowledgement uses a ClOrdID-based form, reports driven by the engine carry an engine-generated identifier, and an Order Status answer (`150=I`) repeats the ExecID of the cached report.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **ExecID: venue differences**
>
> | | US Perps | Spot | Futures |
> | :- | :- | :- | :- |
> | Format | Opaque; three forms, see above | Base32 of a ClOrdID-derived value plus a timestamp | Same as Spot |

**150 - ExecType** `char` *required*

Nature of the event this execution report describes.

**Possible values:**

* `0` : New - the order is live in the engine.
* `4` : Canceled - the order was cancelled.
* `5` : Replace - the amend was applied.
* `8` : Rejected - the matching engine refused an order it had already acknowledged, for example a post-only order that would cross, a fill-or-kill order that cannot be filled in full or an order stopped by market price protection. The report carries OrdRejReason (103) and Text (58). A request refused before it is acknowledged (an unsupported feature, an invalid field, a risk limit) arrives as a BusinessMessageReject (35=j) instead. Engine limit checks (quantity cap, price bounds and bands) map to OrdRejReason `3`, so they may arrive as `150=8` rather than `35=j`; this is to be confirmed in UAT.
* `C` : Expired - the order reached its expiry.
* `D` : Restated - the engine changed the order itself, for example by re-displaying an iceberg slice.
* `F` : Trade - the order got a fill. LastPx/LastQty carry that fill.
* `I` : Order Status - this report answers an OrderStatusRequest rather than reporting an event.

**39 - OrdStatus** `char` *required*

Current state of the order.

**Possible values:**

* `0` : New - live, nothing filled.
* `1` : Partially filled - live with fills against it.
* `2` : Filled - fully filled and closed.
* `4` : Canceled - cancelled and closed.
* `8` : Rejected - the matching engine refused the order after acknowledging it. See OrdRejReason (103) and Text (58) for the reason.
* `C` : Expired - expired and closed.

**103 - OrdRejReason** `integer`

Code identifying the reason why an order was rejected by the matching engine. Present only on Execution Reports with ExecType (150) = 8 and OrdStatus (39) = 8. The gateway maps only the values below; match-time rejects use `3`, `11` and `99`. Which engine rejections reach `150=8` is decided by the engine.

**Possible values:**

* `1` : Unknown symbol.
* `2` : Exchange closed.
* `3` : Order exceeds a limit (quantity cap, price bounds and bands).
* `5` : Unknown order.
* `6` : Duplicate order.
* `11` : Unsupported order characteristic.
* `13` : Incorrect quantity.
* `99` : Other.

The other FIX 4.4 values are not sent.

Required: Conditional

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Engine rejects: venue differences**
>
> | | US Perps | Spot | Futures |
> | :- | :- | :- | :- |
> | Matching-engine reject | Execution Report `150=8` / `39=8` with OrdRejReason (103) | Not sent; arrives as `35=j` | Not sent; arrives as `35=j` |

**1 - Account** `string`

Numeric end-client id of the end client the order was placed for, as sent in Account (1) on the order.

**Format:** Non-zero numeric end-client id.

**55 - Symbol** `string`

Perpetual futures contract, in the venue symbol form. Ten contracts are listed at launch.

**Format:** Venue symbol.

**48 - InstrumentID** `string`

Identifier of the instrument within the Kraken instrument scheme. Present when it is cached for the order.

**Format:** Venue instrument identifier.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **InstrumentID: venue differences**
>
> | | US Perps | Spot | Futures |
> | :- | :- | :- | :- |
> | InstrumentID (48) on reports | Sent when cached | Never sent; InstrumentID is ignored outside US Perps | Same as Spot |

**54 - Side** `char` *required*

Side of the order.

**Possible values:**

* `1` : Buy.
* `2` : Sell.

**38 - OrderQty** `Qty`

Order quantity in contracts.

**40 - OrdType** `char` *required*

Execution model of the order.

**Possible values:**

* `1` : Market - fill the whole quantity immediately at the best available prices.
* `2` : Limit - rest in the book, trading only at the limit price or better.

**44 - Price** `Price`

Limit price of the order, denominated in the quote currency. Required for limit orders.

**Format:** Limit price, denominated in the quote currency.

Required: Conditional

**59 - TimeInForce** `char`

How long the order stays in effect. GTC, IOC, FOK and GTD are the supported durations; a change of TimeInForce on an amend is rejected.

**Possible values:**

* `1` : GTC - good till cancelled. Rests indefinitely.
* `3` : IOC - fill what you can immediately, cancel the rest.
* `4` : FOK - fill the entire quantity immediately or cancel the whole order.
* `6` : GTD - good till date. The expiry is in ExpireTime (126).

**32 - LastQty** `Qty`

Quantity bought or sold on this fill.

Required: Conditional

**31 - LastPx** `Price`

Price of this fill.

Required: Conditional

**151 - LeavesQty** `Qty` *required*

Quantity still working in the book.

**14 - CumQty** `Qty` *required*

Total quantity filled on the order so far.

**381 - GrossTradeAmt** `Amt`

Gross amount traded on the order. The New (`150=0`) acknowledgement carries `0`.

**1138 - DisplayQty** `Qty`

May be present on reports. Display quantity cannot be set on US Perps orders: NewOrderSingle rejects iceberg orders.

**6 - AvgPx** `Price` *required*

Average price across all fills on the order.

**60 - TransactTime** `UTCTimestamp`

Time of the business event in UTC, formatted YYYYMMDD-HH:MM:SS.uuu.

**Format:** UTC timestamp, YYYYMMDD-HH:MM:SS.uuu

**58 - Text** `string`

Free-text description: the cancellation reason, reject reason, or order status detail.

**Format:** Free text set by Kraken; carries the reject or cancellation reason.

**851 - LastLiquidityInd** `integer`

Code indicating whether this fill added liquidity to or removed liquidity from the market. Only `1` and `2` are sent.

**Possible values:**

* `1` : The fill added liquidity to the order book.
* `2` : The fill took liquidity from the order book.

Required: Conditional

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Liquidity indicator: venue differences**
>
> | | US Perps | Spot | Futures |
> | :- | :- | :- | :- |
> | Where it is carried | Tag 851 (`1` or `2`) | Tag 5050, not 851 | Same as Spot |

**1003 - TradeID** `string`

Exchange-assigned identifier of the trade.

Required: Conditional

**trailer** *required*
