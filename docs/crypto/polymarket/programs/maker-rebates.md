# Maker Rebates Program

> Earn daily pUSD rebates by providing liquidity on Polymarket

Polymarket charges taker fees across multiple market categories. Fees are determined by the protocol at match time and fund a **Maker Rebates** program that pays daily pUSD rebates to liquidity providers.

***

## Why Maker Rebates

Deeper liquidity means tighter spreads, lower price impact, more reliable fills, and greater resilience during volatility. Maker Rebates incentivize **consistent, competitive quoting** so everyone gets a better trading experience.

***

## How Maker Rebates Work

* **Paid daily in pUSD:** Rebates are calculated and distributed every day.
* **Performance-based:** Your maker tier determines the percentage of taker fees generated by your eligible fills that you earn.

### Eligibility

Place orders that add liquidity to the book and get filled (i.e., your liquidity is taken by another trader). Standard rebates start at \$200,000 in 30-day maker weighted volume.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Maker rebates are funded by taker fees. Combos and fills on markets with a 0%
> taker fee (including markets temporarily made fee-free during a fee holiday)
> earn **no maker weighted volume and no maker rebate**.

### Payment

Rebates are paid daily in pUSD, directly to your wallet. A minimum accrued rebate of **\$1 pUSD** is required for a payout.

***

## Read Your Rebated Fees

Read a maker's rebate amounts for a calendar day to reconcile the program with
your accounting.

> [tab] TypeScript
>
> Call `fetchCurrentRebates()` using an existing `PublicClient` named `client`.
> If needed, [create a public client](https://docs.polymarket.com/getting-started/typescript.md#create-a-public-client).
> The method is also available on `SecureClient`.
>
> ```ts theme={null}
> const rebates = await client.fetchCurrentRebates({
>   date: "2026-10-06",
>   makerAddress: "0xFeA4cB3dD4ca7CefD3368653B7D6FF9BcDFca604",
> });
>
> for (const rebate of rebates) {
>   console.log(rebate.conditionId, rebate.rebatedFeesUsdc);
> }
> ```
>
> Each `RebatedFee` includes `date`, `conditionId`, `assetAddress`,
> `makerAddress`, and `rebatedFeesUsdc`. The amount is an exact USDC decimal string.
> Keep it as a string or use a decimal library for accounting. The method returns
> an empty array when the maker has no rebates for the requested date.

> [tab] Python
>
> Call `get_current_rebates()` using an existing `AsyncPublicClient` named `client`.
> If needed, [create a public client](https://docs.polymarket.com/getting-started/python.md#create-a-public-client).
> The method is also available on `AsyncSecureClient`. The synchronous clients
> provide the same method.
>
> ```python theme={null}
> rebates = await client.get_current_rebates(
>     date="2026-10-06",
>     maker_address="0xFeA4cB3dD4ca7CefD3368653B7D6FF9BcDFca604",
> )
>
> for rebate in rebates:
>     print(rebate.condition_id, rebate.rebated_fees_usdc)
> ```
>
> Each `RebatedFee` includes `date`, `condition_id`, `asset_address`,
> `maker_address`, and `rebated_fees_usdc`. The date is a `datetime.date` and the
> USDC amount is a `Decimal`. The method returns an empty tuple when the maker
> has no rebates for the requested date.

## Funding

Maker Rebates are funded by taker fees collected in eligible markets. A percentage of these fees are redistributed to makers who keep the markets liquid. The rebate percentage depends on your maker weighted volume over the last 30 days.

| Tier | 30-Day Maker Weighted Volume | Rebate on Taker Fees Generated by Your Fills |
| - | - | - |
| None | Under \$200,000 | 0% |
| Tier 1 | \$200,000 to under \$1 million | 3% |
| Tier 2 | \$1 million to under \$4 million | 7% |
| Tier 3 | \$4 million to under \$10 million | 10% |
| Tier 4 | \$10 million and above | 15% |

These are the standard tier rates. An applicable maker rebate campaign can replace the standard rate.

You can look up maker tiers, rebate rates, and 30-day weighted volume for up to 100 maker addresses without authentication. See [Get maker tiers for addresses](https://docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/rebates/get-maker-tiers-for-addresses.md).

> [!NOTE]
>
> Polymarket collects taker fees in eligible markets across all fee-enabled
> categories. The rebate percentage is at the sole discretion of Polymarket and
> may change over time.

## Tiered Maker Rebates

Your maker tier uses your eligible maker weighted volume over the last 30 days:

```text theme={null}
wV = C × p × (1 - p)
```

Where **C** = number of shares filled and **p** = fill price in dollars. Volume
is added across eligible markets, including BTC 5-minute and 15-minute markets,
and your tier updates daily. Only filled quantities count; there are no category
multipliers. Maker fills and taker fills count toward separate tiers.

For each filled maker order:

```text theme={null}
fee_equivalent = C × feeRate × p × (1 - p)
```

Where **C** = number of shares traded and **p** = price of the shares. The fee parameters differ by market type:

| Category | Taker Fee Rate | Maker Fee Rate |
| - | - | - |
| Crypto — BTC 5-minute and 15-minute up/down | 0.05 | 0 |
| Crypto — all other markets | 0.01 | 0 |
| Sports, including NFL and college football | 0.05 | 0 |
| Finance | 0.05 | 0 |
| Tech | 0.05 | 0 |
| Politics — elections | 0.05 | 0 |
| Politics — excluding elections and geopolitics | 0.01 | 0 |
| Economics | 0.01 | 0 |
| Culture | 0.01 | 0 |
| Weather | 0.01 | 0 |
| Mentions | 0.01 | 0 |
| Geopolitics | 0 | 0 |
| Combos | 0.05 | 0 |
| Other / General | 0.05 | 0 |

Your rebate for each eligible fill:

```text theme={null}
rebate = fee_equivalent × maker_tier_rebate_rate
```

For each eligible fill, use the taker fee before any taker-tier rebate. Your maker rebate depends on your maker tier, not your own taker tier or the counterparty's taker tier. For example, a Tier 4 maker whose eligible fills generate \$100 in taker fees earns \$15.

***

## Taker Fee Structure

Taker fees are calculated in pUSD and vary based on the share price. The fee amount in pUSD is symmetric around 50% probability — a trade at 30¢ incurs the same dollar fee as a trade at 70¢.

### Fee Tables (100 Shares)

For detailed fee tables for each market category, see the [Fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees.md) page.

### Fee Precision

Fees are rounded to 5 decimal places. The smallest fee charged is 0.00001 pUSD. Anything smaller rounds to zero, so very small trades near the extremes may incur no fee at all.

***

## Which Markets Are Eligible

The following market categories have taker fees enabled and are eligible for maker rebates: Crypto, Sports, Finance, Politics, Economics, Culture, Weather, Tech, Mentions, and Other / General. Only fills that generate taker fees qualify. Combos are excluded. Maker rebates are separate from [Liquidity Rewards](https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/liquidity-rewards.md); a market does not need a liquidity rewards allocation for its eligible fills to count.

To confirm whether fees apply to a specific market, see [Trading
Fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-data/market-details.md#trading-fees) in Market Details.

## FAQ

> [accordion] How do I qualify for maker rebates
>
> Place orders that add liquidity to the book and get filled (i.e., your
> liquidity is taken by another trader). Standard rebates start at \$200,000
> in 30-day maker weighted volume.

> [accordion] When are rebates paid
>
> Daily, in pUSD. You must accrue at least \$1 in rebates before a payout is
> issued.

> [accordion] How are rebates calculated
>
> Rebates are your maker tier's percentage of the taker fees generated by your
> eligible fills, before any taker-tier rebate. Your own taker tier and the
> counterparty's taker tier do not affect your maker rebate.

> [accordion] Where do maker rebates come from
>
> Taker fees collected in eligible markets fund maker rebates, which are
> calculated on your own eligible fills and distributed daily.

> [accordion] Which markets have fees enabled
>
> Crypto, Sports, Finance, Politics, Economics, Culture, Weather, Tech,
> Mentions, Other / General, and Combos markets. Combos charge fees but do not
> earn maker rebates.
