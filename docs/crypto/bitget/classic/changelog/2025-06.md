# June 2025

## [June 17, 2025] Spot merged trading depth, spot trading depth, futures merged depth interfaces: ts field adjustment

Interface:/api/v2/mix/market/merge-depth,/api/v2/spot/market/orderbook,/api/v2/spot/market/merge-depth

Changes:
- Spot merged trading depth, spot trading depth, and futures merged depth interfaces: ts field adjusted to matching engine timestamp

## [June 16, 2025] The ADL ranking interface has added the position direction.

Interface:/api/v2/mix/position/adlRank <br/>
Changes:
- The ADL ranking interface has added the position direction `holdSide` field。

## [June 9, 2025] Get Contract Information Adds Maximum Order Quantity Fields

Interface:/api/v2/mix/market/contracts <br/>
Changes:
-  Added maxMarketOrderQty field for the maximum quantity of a single market order. 
-  Added maxOrderQty field for the maximum quantity of a single limit order.

# 2025 年 6 月

## [6月17日, 2025] 现货获取合并交易深度、现货获取交易深度、合约合并深度接口 ts字段调整

接口：/api/v2/mix/market/merge-depth,/api/v2/spot/market/orderbook,/api/v2/spot/market/merge-depth

调整内容：
- 现货获取合并交易深度、现货获取交易深度、合约合并深度接口 ts字段调整为撮合引擎时间戳

## [6月16日, 2025] ADL排名接口新增持仓方向

接口：/api/v2/mix/position/adlRank <br/>
新增内容：
- ADL排名接口新增持仓方向 `holdSide` 字段。

## [6月9日, 2025] 获取合约信息接口新增最大下单数量字段

接口：/api/v2/mix/market/contracts <br/>
新增内容：
-  新增单笔市价单最大下单数量字段`maxMarketOrderQty`，单笔限价单最大下单数量字段`maxOrderQty`。
